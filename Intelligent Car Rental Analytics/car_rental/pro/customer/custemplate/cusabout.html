Welcome to Car Rental

This Car Rental E-Commerce Site is an advanced web application designed to facilitate the booking and management of rental vehicles through a seamless online platform. Developed using Python and Django, this project aims to provide a robust, user-friendly system for both customers and administrators.

As the demand for convenient and flexible transportation options continues to rise, our solution aims to bridge the gap between customers and vehicle rental services. The site is crafted to handle various aspects of the rental process, from browsing available cars to managing bookings and processing payments, all within a single, integrated system.

Developed by: Darshan Prajapati, Parv Patel, and Nishtha Patel
